# AURELIA Product Image Guide — Part 1 of 3

### Total: 18 products, ~60 images

**GLOBAL RULES (all prompts):**
- Young Indian female model, 22–28, natural skin, minimal makeup
- Full-frame DSLR, 85mm, f/1.8–f/2.8, shallow DOF
- **3:4 portrait** (768×1024 or 1024×1365)
- Full-length, entire garment visible
- Photorealistic catalogue — NOT CGI, NOT illustration
- No text, no watermark, no logo, no collage
